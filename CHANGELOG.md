# Changelog

## v26.10.1 (2026-10-26)

MoAir now carries its open-source credits and a way to reach us.

- About MoAir in the menu bar opens the About panel, with credits for the open-source code inside.
- The full third-party notices ship in the app and beside it in the disk image.
- A Contact submenu reaches support, feedback and security reports.
- The app and its disk image are signed and notarized.
